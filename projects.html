Custom iOS Social Coffee Platform

Just Bean It.

Social media coffee app

Just Bean It is a fully custom-built social coffee platform designed and developed entirely by me, from initial concept and UI/UX design to backend architecture and TestFlight deployment. The app was created as a personal passion project to combine social networking, coffee culture, and location-based discovery into a modern mobile experience.

Built using SwiftUI and Supabase, Just Bean It leverages a scalable real-time backend architecture with authentication, database management, storage, and social interactions fully integrated into the application. The project was developed from scratch without templates, allowing complete ownership over the product vision, technical implementation, and user experience.

Key Features

01 Social Coffee Feed

Photo-based coffee reviews built around sharing real coffee experiences.

02 User Profiles

Profiles with a followers and friends system for social discovery.

03 Account Privacy

Public and private account functionality with follow request support.

04 Coffee Shop Discovery

Apple Maps integration for finding nearby coffee shops.

05 Verified Reviews

Location-based post verification for more authentic coffee reviews.

06 Map Exploration

Interactive map browsing with directions support.

07 Badges & Achievements

Custom badge and achievement system tailored to coffee activity.

08 Editable Identity

Profile customization with editable avatars.

09 Real-Time Interactions

Comments, notifications, and follow requests integrated into the app.

10 Search History

Search system with recent search history for faster revisits.

11 Multi-Photo Uploads

Cloud storage integration for multiple post photos and media handling.

12 Custom UI

Responsive glassmorphism-inspired interface with custom theming.

What makes Just Bean It stand out is its focus on creating a dedicated social platform specifically for coffee enthusiasts rather than trying to be a generic review app. The app combines social networking, local discovery, visual storytelling, and coffee culture into a polished mobile-first experience. Features like verified location-based reviews, coffee-focused achievements, map-driven exploration, and social engagement systems create a niche community platform tailored to a passionate audience.

This project demonstrates end-to-end product ownership including product strategy, mobile development, backend engineering, database design, authentication flows, cloud storage architecture, UI/UX design, deployment, and App Store/TestFlight distribution.
