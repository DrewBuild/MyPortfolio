Just Bean It

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Just Bean It handles information used to provide account features, coffee posts, cafe discovery, social interactions, and app support.

Information Collected

Just Bean It may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, username, email address, profile details, coffee posts, reviews, comments, saved items, and app feedback.

Account Data

Account and profile information is used to create your account, show your profile, support social features, and help secure access to the app. Authentication and user records are handled through Supabase.

Coffee Posts & Photos

When you upload coffee photos or create posts, that content may be stored with Supabase storage and database services so it can appear in the app feed, profiles, cafe pages, collections, and related features.

Location Usage

Location access is optional and may be used for cafe discovery, nearby search, maps, directions, and visit verification. You can manage location permissions through your device settings.

Analytics

The app may use limited analytics or diagnostic information to understand app performance, fix bugs, and improve core features. This information is used to maintain and improve Just Bean It.

Contact Information

If you contact support, your email address and message contents may be used to respond to your request and track app feedback or reported bugs.

Data Storage

Just Bean It uses Supabase backend services for authentication, database records, and uploaded media storage. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect app data, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request help accessing, updating, or deleting account-related information by contacting support. Some data may be retained when required for security, legal, or operational reasons.
